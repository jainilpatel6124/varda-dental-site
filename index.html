Travel tips for dental care

Check credentials

Choose a dentist with proper degrees and experience. Our doctors are MDS qualified.

Share your records

Send recent X-rays and photos before travelling so a plan can be prepared in advance.

Get the plan in writing

Ask for the treatment steps, number of visits and cost before you book flights.

Plan time to heal

Keep a few spare days for healing and follow-up checks, rather than rushing home.

Ask about aftercare

Confirm how you can reach the clinic for advice after you return.

FAQ

Common Questions

Where is Varda Dental located in Gandhinagar?

Varda Dental Aesthetics & Implant Center is on the Ground Floor, Swagat Rainforest-3, Block-A, 7/8, Sargasan, Gandhinagar, Gujarat 382421. It is easy to reach from Kudasan, Raysan, Infocity, GIFT City and Ahmedabad.

What are the clinic timings?

Monday to Saturday: 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Sunday: 10:00 AM to 12:30 PM (IST).

How much does a dental implant cost at Varda Dental?

An Osstem (South Korea) implant starts at ₹30,000 per implant. The crown on the implant is charged separately, starting from ₹8,000. Your dentist confirms the final cost after examination.

How much do braces and clear aligners cost?

Metal braces start from ₹35,000, ceramic braces from ₹40,000, clear aligners from ₹1,00,000 and Invisalign from ₹1,70,000. The exact cost depends on your case.

Do you offer online consultation?

Yes. The online consultation fee is ₹500. Message us on WhatsApp to book a time.

Is dental treatment painful?

We use gentle techniques and modern anesthesia so most treatments are comfortable. Tell us if you feel anxious and we will go slowly.

How long do clear aligners take?

It depends on your teeth, commonly several months. We share an estimate after your consultation.

Do you treat children?

Yes, we offer gentle pediatric dentistry for kids of all ages.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call +91 81409 97682 right away. We prioritize severe pain, swelling and injuries.
